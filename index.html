What it does

A discount that belongs to one invoice — not to a customer or a template.

You see the invoice amount first, then decide. Time entries, rates and budgets stay untouched.

🧾

Multiple discount lines

Up to 10 lines per invoice, each with its own description and either a fixed net amount or a percentage.

⚡

Entered where you invoice

Correct totals everywhere

Calculation

One clear rule: one after another, on the remaining net amount.

  • Each line is applied in order to what is left after the previous one; taxes are calculated afterwards.
  • Calculated in whole cents, percentages rounded half-up. The discount lines always add up exactly.
  • Without a discount, the plugin calculates exactly like Kimai’s own calculator.

Quick start

Install, pick a calculator, discount your next invoice.

  1. 1Unzip the plugin into var/plugins/ so that var/plugins/DiscountBundle/ exists, then run the commands on the right.
  2. 2In Invoices → Invoice template, set the grouping of invoice lines to “Default + discount” or “Project + discount”.
  3. 3On Invoices → Create invoice, click “+ Discount”, add your lines and save the invoice.
Install commands
unzip DiscountBundle.zip -d var/plugins/
bin/console kimai:reload
bin/console kimai:bundle:discount:install

Run as the web server user (e.g. www-data). Works in Docker with kimai/kimai2.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I have to change my invoice template?+

No. Discount lines are part of entries, and invoice.subtotal, invoice.tax_rows, invoice.total and invoice.total_plain already include them. Only custom documents that add up totals on their own need an update — and the discount must never be subtracted again in Twig.

Does it change time entries, rates or budgets?+

No. Worked time, rates, internal rates and budgets stay as they are. That is also why Kimai’s time-entry based reports don’t include the discount — the invoice history, its export and the plugin’s own report do.

What happens when I select a discount with another calculator?+

Kimai rejects the preview and saving with a clear message. A discount is never ignored silently.

Can I uninstall it later?+

Yes. Switch your invoice templates back to Kimai’s calculators and disable the plugin. Saved invoices, their amounts, PDFs and snapshots stay unchanged; the plugin never deletes data automatically.
